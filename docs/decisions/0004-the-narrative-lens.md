# 0004: The Narrative Lens

**Kind:** decision
**Links:** #853, #743, #744, #870

## Ruling

Source: https://github.com/pythagorakase/nexus/issues/853#issuecomment-5565300043.

> **Ruling (owner, 2026-09-07).** No persisted per-story lens. Point of view and tense are fixed constants for every story: second person, present tense. Texture, register, and dialogue density are not stored per story; the writer adapts them to the genre and to the player's input and preferences, and when in doubt errs toward more dialogue. Three input-handling rules for the writer seat (filed as their own issue): (1) a paraphrased intent ("I try to convince him to drop the investigation") is a cue for Skald to author the PC's dialogue so it honors that intention and fits the PC's existing voice; (2) input written in the PC's voice is usually incorporated into the scene as dialogue; (3) for a lengthy speech, writing the scene as the world's reaction to it, rather than reproducing it verbatim, is a viable option. C002 (#743) closes as not planned; C004 (#744) loses its C002 dependency and proceeds as a compile-time selection policy per its verifier amendment.

## Rejected Alternatives

- "Should each story carry an explicit, persisted lens (viewpoint, tense, psychic distance, narrator presence, register) distinct from the setting artifact". No reason recorded.
